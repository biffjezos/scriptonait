Scriptonait

A small transformer, trained from scratch on your own film scripts, stories, or books — entirely in your browser, generation accelerated by WebGPU.

Only works when the site allows cross-origin fetches (CORS) — most don't. If it fails, copy the text and use "Paste text" below instead.

Plain-text formats only (.txt, .md, .fountain, ...) — not .pdf or .docx.

No sources yet — add one above to start building your training corpus.

2. Model shape

"Nodes" is the model's width — bigger learns more, costs more memory. Attention window smaller than context length uses cheaper sliding-window attention, good for long scripts/books; leave it equal to context length for plain full attention.
